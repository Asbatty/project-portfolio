LED Matrix

I'm building a 64×32 RGB LED matrix into a 3D printed frame, with an ESP32 driving it.

Type Personal project
Date 2026 (in progress)
Role CAD, electronics, code

Overview

The panel is 64×32 RGB, which is 2,048 LEDs at a 4 mm pitch, roughly 10 by 5 inches. An ESP32 drives it from inside a frame that holds the panel and electronics. It sits on a bookshelf and cycles through a clock, animated art, and a stick figure acting out the current weather. Home Assistant sends it messages and alerts over MQTT.

The app

The frame serves its own phone app. It shows a live view of the panel, a gallery of faces with real previews, settings for each face, and the rotation order.
