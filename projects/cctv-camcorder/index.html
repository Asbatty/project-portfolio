CCTV Camcorder

A CCTV security camera turned into a handheld camcorder.

Type Personal project
Date 2026 (in progress)
Role Mechanical design, electronics

Overview

I had an old CCTV camera and wanted to see if I could turn it into something I'd actually carry around and shoot with. Security cameras are made to sit on a wall, plugged into wall power and a recorder somewhere else, so most of the work here is making all of that portable.

How it goes together

I designed a carrying handle and a mount for the LCD screen so I can frame shots while holding it. Everything runs off one 12V LiPo pack. The plan is for the camera's analog video to go over BNC to a mini DVR for recording and to the LCD so I can see what I'm shooting.

Before mounting anything I laid it all out on the bench and checked the power wiring and connections with a multimeter, so I knew the camera, screen, and recorder would all run off the battery.

Where it's at

This one is still in progress. The photo below is from bench testing. Next up is mounting everything to the handle and taking it out to shoot.
